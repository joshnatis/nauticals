Stuff happens

I was just finishing scrubbing the galley table in the James Charles to the point where I could see my own reflection in it, when a rumor whizzed right by my ear. We were headed to the yard in Fall River right away, moving all of our shit to the Maureen, and starting a very long journey to some undisclosed location for a job. I couldn't discern whether this was true or just a joke, but soon enough we had reached my old stomping grounds at the yard.
